About Me



Namaskara! I'm Vinay Karthik M B, a Bengaluru-based Senior Software Engineer with 4+ years of experience in full-stack development, AI solutions, Identity and Access Management (IAM), cloud infrastructure, and enterprise software architecture.

I build secure, practical products with PHP, Python, Java, Kotlin, Laravel, the MERN stack, Flutter, AWS, OpenAI APIs, WSO2 Identity Server, and modern DevOps practices. At Attica Gold, I lead engineering delivery, architecture planning, code reviews, hiring, and stakeholder collaboration while developing digital-gold platforms, internal business systems, AI automation, IoT monitoring, and secure SSO/MFA solutions.

My work focuses on measurable business impact. An in-house attendance management platform I developed replaced third-party software and reduced annual operating costs by more than ₹15 lakh. I have also delivered AI-powered customer detection, voice and transcription tools, mobile applications, payment integrations, HRMS and operations dashboards, and scalable cloud deployments.

Previously, I built IAM solutions at Trinity Mobility and delivered web applications, infrastructure, SEO, and automation for freelance clients across hospitality, construction, travel, and entertainment. I hold an MSc in Informatics from the University of Miskolc, where I received the Bronze Medallion of Merit, and a BE in Computer Science and Engineering.
